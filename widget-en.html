Quick guide

Meeting Rooms - Cisco

Share content

Cable (recommended)

  1. Connect USB-C to your computer
  2. Tap Share in meeting on the touch panel

Wireless

  1. Tap Share on the touch panel, then Webex Share
  2. Open share.webex.com in the browser on your computer
  3. Enter the 9-digit sharing code shown on the touch panel
  4. A 4-digit PIN now appears in the top right corner of the room screen
  5. Enter the PIN on your computer
  6. Select ”Share screen” and choose what to display
