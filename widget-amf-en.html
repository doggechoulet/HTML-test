Quick guide

Meeting Rooms - Cisco

Video meeting from your computer

  1. Connect the USB-C cable to your computer
  2. Start the meeting in your own app, such as Teams or Zoom
  3. Select Cisco Room Bar as camera, microphone and speaker

Share content

Via cable (recommended)

  1. Connect the USB-C cable to your computer
  2. Your screen is shared automatically

Wireless with AirPlay (Mac, iPhone, iPad)

  1. Open Control Center and select Screen Mirroring
  2. Select the room name from the list

Wireless with Miracast (Windows)

  1. Press the Windows key + K
  2. Select the room name from the list
